Week 2.2 · The 2×2
Four boxes, two questions
A SWOT analysis sorts everything a company faces by asking two questions about each fact. Question 1: is this inside the company (something Nike controls) or outside (something happening TO Nike)? Question 2: is it helping or hurting?

          Week 2.2 · Group Consensus
          ✋ STOP — Turn to your group. Compare grids out loud before you touch anything below.
          Two of the twelve facts don't sort cleanly — groups always split on them. Your group must agree on a final box for each, and then write down the rule you used to decide. One answer per person; your group should agree on the wording together.
          "Sales in Greater China have fallen eight quarters in a row."
          Is that the market turning against Nike (external), or Nike failing to execute in China (internal)?
          "Running is booming — Nike's running category grew more than 20% for three straight quarters."
          Is the boom Nike's doing (internal strength), or an outside wave Nike is riding (external opportunity)?
          Write your group's rule: how do you decide whether a fact is INTERNAL or EXTERNAL? One or two sentences — a test you could apply to any company, not just Nike.
