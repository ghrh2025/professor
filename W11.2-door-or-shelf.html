Step 3 · Year 1 · As a group
Year 1. Find the money.
As a group About 6 minutes
  1. Go in order, Role 1 first. Each person reads their private facts and opening argument out loud. 30 seconds each.
  2. Set the plan together: pick a fee and a markup below. Everyone in the group enters the same plan on their own screen.
  3. The Member decides. Role 3 says out loud whether she renews under this plan. If she says no, change the plan and ask again.
  4. Then each person types what their role thinks of the final plan.
