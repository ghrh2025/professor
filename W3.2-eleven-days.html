Week 3.2 · Eleven Days, Five Steps
Every buyer walks the same five steps
Maya Ruiz took eleven days. Some people take eleven minutes. The steps are the same either way, and the sale is almost never won or lost at the one everybody watches.
STEP 1

STEP 2

STEP 5
Postpurchase
You explain it to yourself
Two phases. First, nine moments from the case come at you one at a time. Put each one on the step where it belongs. You will not be told whether you are right, and that is on purpose. Second, your group reads its own timeline, decides which step CarMax won and which step lost her, and names one change CarMax could make. You may not cut the price.
Say your answer out loud to your group before you tap. If someone disagrees, argue it. The tap is the end of the argument, not the start of it.
