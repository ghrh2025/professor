Week 6.2 · Positioning
Monday you found the cliff. Today the map becomes a sentence.
Buyers doubted the cheapest glasses, interest climbed with price, and it fell off a cliff at $100. Warby Parker launched at $95. A map shows where you are. A positioning statement says it in words a competitor cannot copy.
For [target], Warby Parker is the [frame of reference] that [point of difference], because [reason to believe].
Target segmentThe specific group of people you serve.
Frame of referenceThe category you compete in, and who you get compared against.
Point of differenceThe reason this target picks you over the others in that frame.
Reason to believeThe proof that makes the difference true, not just claimed.
