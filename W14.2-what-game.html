Week 14.2 · Round 1
Round 1: Trade
Read the rules with your group. The professor starts the clock.
01
Goal: finish with the most Skittles of your color.
02
Trade anything for anything. One for one, two for one, whatever you and the other person agree to.
03
Every trade goes through the professor. Bring it to the front. It only counts once he approves it.
04
Every approved trade costs one Skittle, paid to the professor. You and your partner decide who pays. Any color.
05
No eating until the professor says the exercise is over.
Keep count as you go: how many trades you make, and how many Skittles you pay. You will enter both when time is called.

Week 14.2 · Round 2
Round 2: Your call
Same goal. This round you choose how to play, and you can change your mind at any time.
A
Keep trading through the professor. Same rules, same one-Skittle fee.
B
Trade directly with anyone. No approval and no fee.
C
Stop. Keep what you have and sit the round out.
Still no eating. Keep counting trades and fees.

Week 14.2 · Before the reveal
STOP. Turn to your group.

Put your piles next to each other. Compare where you started with where you ended. Then answer these three on your own.

1. Who will end today with the most Skittles of any single color?
2. Before Round 1 started, did you ask why the goal was the most of one color?
3. Name one game in your own life where someone else wrote the rules and gets paid on every move you make. Who is the house?
