Step 3 · Trade facts, out loud
Read your facts. Earn the code words.
As a group About 3 minutes
  1. Go in order, Role 1 first. Read your private facts and your opening argument out loud. About 30 seconds each.
  2. When you finish reading, say your code word. Not before.
  3. Type the other roles' code words below. The plan does not open until you have heard every role.
  4. Missing a role? Tap “Nobody in our group has this role” and read that role's facts out loud yourselves.

Step 4 · Build the plan · As a group
Four days. Nine pieces. Decide.
As a group About 6 minutes
  1. Decide each piece together: keep doing it, stop doing it, or make it ask for the doll. Everyone enters the same plan on their own screen.
  2. You must stop at least one piece. For each one you stop, say which other piece is already doing its job.
  3. You may change two pieces at most so they ask for the doll. Four days is not long. For each one, pick the tool and write what it says.
  4. The Parent decides. Role 3 says out loud whether anything in this plan asks her to buy a doll. If she says no, change the plan and ask again, or accept her answer as a group.
