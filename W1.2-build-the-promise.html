Week 1.2 · The Template
One promise, five pieces
On Monday you met the five ingredients of a value proposition. A slogan ("Murder Your Thirst") is not one. A value proposition names who it's for and what it promises. Here's the template you'll fill in:
For [who it's for] who [their problem], we offer [what we give them] so they can [the job it does] — unlike [the alternative].
Work as a group. Split the five slots across your team — each person champions at least one and has to defend their pick out loud before the group locks it in. There's a strong answer for each slot and two that miss. Argue it out.
